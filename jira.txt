jira:
description:
-------------
Jira is a project management and issue-tracking tool.It is widely used
by Agile teams to plan, track, and manage tasks, bugs, and workflows
efficiently.
Flow representation:
--------------------
Step 1: Start
Step 2: Create project in Jira
Step 3: Add issues (tasks/bugs/stories)
Step 4: Assign issues to team members
Step 5: Plan sprint (if Agile)
Step 6: Work on assigned tasks
Step 7: Update status (To Do → In Progress → Done)
Step 8: Review and test completed work
Step 9: Track progress using boards/reports
Step 10: Close issues
Step 11: End
