Team collaboration:
-------------------
Description:
------------
Team collaboration is the process where team members work together, 
share ideas, and communicate effectively to achieve a common goal.
Flow Representation:
--------------------
Step 1: Start
Step 2: Define common goal
Step 3: Assign roles and responsibilities
Step 4: Share ideas and plan tasks
Step 5: Communicate regularly
Step 6: Work together on tasks
Step 7: Review progress and give feedback
Step 8: Resolve issues if any
Step 9: Complete the project
Step 10: End
