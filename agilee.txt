1.Agile:
Description:
Agile is a software development methodology that focuses on delivering
work in small, incremental iterations, allowing teams to adapt quickly
to changes. It emphasizes continuous feedback, collaboration, and improvement 
to produce high-quality results efficiently.

flow representation:
---------------------
Step 1: Start

Step 2: Gather requirements from client

Step 3: Break requirements into small tasks (iterations/sprints)

Step 4: Plan the current sprint

Step 5: Design the solution for selected tasks

Step 6: Develop (write code)

Step 7: Test the developed features

Step 8: Deploy the working product

Step 9: Collect feedback from client/users

Step 10: If changes or new requirements exist, go back to Step 3

Step 11: Else, End
