difference between traditional and agile:
-----------------------------------------
| Aspect               | Traditional (Waterfall)     | Agile                          |
| -------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Approach             | Linear, step-by-step        | Iterative, cyclic              |
| Flexibility          | Very low (changes are hard) | High (changes allowed anytime) |
| Delivery             | One final delivery          | Multiple small releases        |
| Customer Involvement | Only at beginning/end       | Continuous involvement         |
| Testing              | Done at the end             | Done in every iteration        |
| Risk                 | Higher (issues found late)  | Lower (issues found early)     |

flow representation for traditional:
-------------------------------------
Step 1: Start
Step 2: Gather all requirements
Step 3: Design the system
Step 4: Develop (coding)
Step 5: Test the complete system
Step 6: Deploy the final product
Step 7: Maintain
Step 8: End

flow representation for agile:
-------------------------------
Step 1: Start
Step 2: Gather initial requirements
Step 3: Divide into small tasks (sprints)
Step 4: Plan sprint
Step 5: Design
Step 6: Develop
Step 7: Test
Step 8: Deploy small feature
Step 9: Collect feedback
Step 10: Repeat from Step 3 until project completion
Step 11: End
