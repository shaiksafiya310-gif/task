scrum:
Description:
--------------
Scrum is an Agile framework used to manage and develop projects through
short cycles called sprints.It focuses on team collaboration,
continuous feedback, and incremental delivery of working products.
flow representation:
--------------------
Step 1: Start
Step 2: Create Product Backlog (list of requirements)
Step 3: Plan Sprint (select tasks from backlog)
Step 4: Start Sprint (2–4 weeks)
Step 5: Daily Scrum Meeting (discuss progress)
Step 6: Develop and Test features
Step 7: Sprint Review (show completed work)
Step 8: Sprint Retrospective (improve process)
Step 9: Update backlog if needed
Step 10: Repeat from Step 3 until project completion
Step 11: End
